Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 2, 2026

AllerScout (小敏守护, the “App”) is a tool for individuals and caregivers to organize allergy information for a family member, compare visible food-label text, and record related events. We respect individual and family privacy. This policy explains what information the App processes, why it is processed, how it is stored, and how you can export or delete it.

At a glance: The App requires no account, displays no third-party advertising, does not track users across apps, and includes no third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. Food-label text recognition and watched-term comparison occur entirely on the device. Profiles and records stay in protected App storage on the current device, are excluded from system cloud backup, and are not synced or uploaded to the developer.

1. Information the App Processes

To provide profiles, food-label analysis, event records, safety cards, medication management, and reminders, the App processes information that you choose to enter or create through use of the App:

  • Profile: a name or nickname, date of birth, confirmed or suspected allergens, source of the information, doctor-provided care-plan text, medication location, and update dates.
  • Health and safety information: the date and time of a reaction, suspected exposure, symptoms, actions taken, doctor notes, medication name, purpose, expiry date, reminder settings, and notes.
  • Emergency contact information: a contact name and telephone number that you enter for safety cards or system telephone calls.
  • Food and scan records: OCR-recognized text, manually corrected text, product name, brand, barcode, notes, selected label-reference region, reference-vocabulary version, and visible-text comparison results.
  • Reaction-record photos: food, package, or symptom photos that you explicitly choose to add are stored on the device as part of that reaction record.
  • App settings: interface language, label-reference region, travel region, selected profile, onboarding status, and other configuration needed for the App to operate.

2. Camera, Photos, and On-Device OCR

The App uses the camera or system photo picker only after you choose to scan a food label. Images are recognized on the device using Apple’s Vision framework and are not sent to a developer-operated server or third-party AI service. Label scanning does not add the original ingredient image to scan history; it saves the visible text you confirm, product information, and text-comparison result. A reaction-record photo is stored only after you explicitly choose it. You can revoke camera access in iOS Settings at any time. Manual label-text entry remains available without camera access.

3. Notifications

If you choose to enable a medication expiry reminder, the App requests notification permission and schedules the notification locally on the device. Notifications are for checking or replacing medication, not advertising or marketing. You can disable an individual reminder in the App or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings.

4. On-Device Storage

The App uses Apple SwiftData to keep profiles, allergy information, label text, scan results, product records, reaction records, medication records, and settings in the App sandbox on the current device. The storage directory uses iOS file protection and is excluded from system cloud backup.

The current version uses no user account, developer-operated server, cloud database, or cross-device synchronization. The developer cannot access profiles or records kept on your device. To use data on another device, you must explicitly export a backup and manually restore it on the destination device.

5. How Information Is Used

The information is used only to provide on-device features you choose to use, including creating profiles, comparing visible label text with watched profile terms and the public reference vocabulary for a selected region, generating history and safety cards, showing emergency information, scheduling local medication reminders, and exporting data. We do not use profile health information for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

6. Sharing, Sale, and Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not share it with data brokers, advertisers, or third-party marketing platforms. Information may be processed by the following recipients only when you use the relevant feature or direct the sharing:

  • Apple system features: provide local notifications, the system photo picker, telephone features, and the system share interface when you explicitly use the relevant feature.
  • Recipients or apps you choose: when you export App data, share a safety card, or send content through the system share sheet, the selected service or recipient will process the content you choose. Review the content before sharing because it may contain personal health or contact information.
  • External reference websites: if you open a medical or official food-label reference link from the App, the destination site may process network-request information under its own policy.

Except for system features, sharing, or external-site visits that you initiate as described above, the App does not transmit user data to the developer or third parties. Recipients, apps, or external sites you choose are governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Family and Minor Privacy

The App is intended for individuals, parents, guardians, and caregivers managing allergy information. Information about a minor should be created and managed by an adult authorized to handle it. We do not ask minors to register a developer account, serve targeted advertising to minors, or track minors across apps. An authorized adult can edit, export, or delete related information in the App.

8. Retention, Export, and Deletion

Data remains only on the current device until you delete particular content in the App, use “Delete All Data,” or delete the App. The App does not keep a cloud copy that the developer can restore.

  • You can edit or delete profiles, medications, products, scans, and reaction records in the App.
  • You can export a copy of App data from Settings and choose where to share it.
  • You can use “Delete All Data” in Settings to remove data managed by the App.
  • Backups, PDFs, or images that you explicitly export or share may remain at the destination you selected or with a recipient and must be deleted separately by you or the recipient.
  • Deleting the App removes its data from the current device. Because App data is excluded from cloud backup, reinstalling the App does not automatically restore these records.

9. Data Security

We use the iOS app sandbox, file protection, and system permission controls to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, and exclude on-device App data from system cloud backup. No storage method is completely secure. Protect your device and passcode, and take care when sharing exports or safety cards containing personal health information.

10. Medical, Regulatory, and Safety Notice

The App is an information-organization and visible-text comparison tool. It does not provide a medical diagnosis or severity assessment, make a regulatory-compliance determination, or guarantee that a food is safe. OCR and reference-term matching may be affected by incomplete labels, image quality, recipe changes, wording differences, or the scope of the reference vocabulary. “No match” does not mean that food is safe. Check the complete original package every time and follow the plan provided by a qualified clinician. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when App features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Where appropriate, material changes may also be explained in the App.
