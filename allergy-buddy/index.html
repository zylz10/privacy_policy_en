Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: July 30, 2026

Allergy Buddy (小敏守护, the “App”) is a tool for parents and caregivers to manage a child’s allergy information, identify potential food-label risks, and record related events. We respect the privacy of children and families. This policy explains what information the App processes, why it is processed, how it is stored, and how you can export or delete it.

At a glance: The App does not require a developer account, display third-party advertising, track users across apps, or include third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. Food-label recognition and risk matching occur on the device. App data is stored on the device and synced through Apple CloudKit to the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account.

1. Information the App Processes

To provide child profiles, food-label analysis, event records, safety cards, medication management, and reminders, the App processes information that you choose to enter or create through use of the App:

  • Child profile: the child’s name or nickname, date of birth, confirmed or suspected allergens, source of the information, doctor-provided care-plan text, medication location, and update dates.
  • Health and safety information: the date and time of a reaction, suspected exposure, symptoms, actions taken, doctor notes, medication name, purpose, expiry date, reminder settings, and notes.
  • Emergency contact information: a contact name and telephone number that you enter for safety cards or system telephone calls.
  • Food and scan records: food ingredient labels that you photograph or select, OCR-recognized text, manually corrected text, product name, brand, barcode, notes, selected regional rules, risk assessment, and matching rationale.
  • App settings: interface language, food-label region, travel region, selected child, onboarding status, sync status, and other configuration needed for the App to operate.
  • Widget data: a limited safety-card snapshot stored in the App Group container on the same device so the App’s widget can display a safety summary.

2. Camera, Photos, and On-Device OCR

The App uses the camera or system photo picker only after you choose to scan a food label. Images are recognized on the device using Apple’s Vision framework and are not sent to a developer-operated server or third-party AI service. The current version does not save the original label image to scan history or iCloud; it saves the text you confirm, product information, and risk result. You can revoke camera access in iOS Settings at any time. Manual ingredient-text entry remains available without camera access.

3. Notifications

If you choose to enable a medication expiry reminder, the App requests notification permission and schedules the notification locally on the device. Notifications are for checking or replacing medication, not advertising or marketing. You can disable an individual reminder in the App or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings.

4. Local Storage and iCloud Sync

The App uses Apple SwiftData to store data on the device and Apple CloudKit to sync child profiles, allergy information, scan results, product records, reaction records, medication records, and certain settings to the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account. Sync may use an internet connection and is subject to your Apple Account, iCloud settings, network conditions, and Apple’s terms.

The developer does not operate a separate server that receives this information and does not use an in-App developer account to identify you. Apple processes the data as the provider of iCloud under its terms and privacy policy. If iCloud is unavailable, the App attempts to continue using data stored locally on the device.

5. How Information Is Used

The information is used only to provide and maintain features you choose to use, including creating profiles, analyzing ingredient text under the selected regional rules, generating history and safety cards, showing emergency information, scheduling medication reminders, exporting data, supporting the widget, and syncing data across your Apple devices. We do not use children’s health information for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

6. Sharing, Sale, and Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not share it with data brokers, advertisers, or third-party marketing platforms. Information may be processed by the following recipients only when you use the relevant feature or direct the sharing:

  • Apple: provides iCloud/CloudKit, system notifications, the system photo picker, telephone features, and system sharing.
  • Recipients or apps you choose: when you export App data, share a safety card, or send content through the system share sheet, the selected service or recipient will process the content you choose. Review the content before sharing because it may contain a child’s health or contact information.
  • External reference websites: if you open a medical or official food-label reference link from the App, the destination site may process network-request information under its own policy.

Except as described above, the App does not provide user data to third parties. Any service provider processing data on our behalf is expected, under its applicable terms and law, to apply protections equivalent to those described in this policy and required by applicable App Store rules. Recipients or external sites that you choose are governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for parents, guardians, and caregivers managing children’s allergy risks. A child’s information should be created and managed by an adult authorized to handle that information. We do not ask children to register a developer account, serve targeted advertising to children, or track children across apps. A parent or guardian can edit, export, or delete related information in the App.

8. Retention, Export, and Deletion

Data remains on the device and in your iCloud until you delete particular content in the App, use “Delete All Data,” remove the App and its local data, or delete the App’s data from iCloud. Because sync may not be immediate, deletion can require an internet connection before it reaches other devices.

  • You can edit or delete child profiles, medications, products, scans, and reaction records in the App.
  • You can export a copy of App data from Settings and choose where to share it.
  • You can use “Delete All Data” in Settings to remove data managed by the App.
  • You can manage or delete the App’s iCloud data through your Apple Account’s iCloud storage settings.
  • Deleting the App removes local data from the current device but does not necessarily delete data that remains in iCloud.

9. Data Security

We use the iOS app sandbox, operating-system storage protection, CloudKit private databases, and system permission controls to reduce the risk of unauthorized access. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device, passcode, and Apple Account, and take care when sharing exports or safety cards containing a child’s health information.

10. Medical and Safety Notice

The App is an information-management and assistive recognition tool. It does not provide a medical diagnosis and cannot guarantee that a food is safe. OCR and rules-based matching may be affected by incomplete labels, image quality, recipe changes, or differences in regional rules. Always check the original package and follow the plan provided by a qualified clinician. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when App features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Where appropriate, material changes may also be explained in the App.
